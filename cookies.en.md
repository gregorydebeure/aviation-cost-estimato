# Cookie policy

## 1. Strictly necessary
To work, the application uses technical session and security identifiers. They are not used for advertising and do not require consent.

## 2. Payment
At payment you are redirected to Stripe's secure page, which sets its own cookies under its own policy.

## 3. Audience measurement
Audience measurement (Google Analytics 4) may be used. It is used only according to your consent choices, and without sending your e-mail, your name or the content of your files.

## 4. Managing your choices
You can delete or block cookies in your browser; blocking strictly necessary cookies may prevent login. Contact: {{contact_email}}.
