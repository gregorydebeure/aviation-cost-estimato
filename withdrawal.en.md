## 1. Principle
There is no blanket “no refund after payment” clause. Accepting the terms does not remove any right of withdrawal you may have: that depends on the law applicable to your purchase and on whether you are an Individual or a Business.

## 2. Depending on what you buy
- **Application subscription (Menkor Pro)**: digital access provided from activation, billed monthly.
- **Time-limited access packs**: digital access provided from activation, for the period stated in the recap.
- **Digital reports**: digital content that you generate and download from the application under an Offer.
- **Aircraft listing**: monthly subscription, published after review.
- **Manual services (Market Search, aircraft-specific budget)**: a service prepared for you from your criteria.

## 3. Consumer right of withdrawal
If the law applicable to your purchase gives you a right of withdrawal as a consumer (for example, in the European Union, for certain distance contracts), you may exercise it within the period set by that law by writing to {{contact_email}} with your order number. The period and its conditions are those of the law that applies to you.

## 4. Request for immediate performance
So that access or the service starts immediately, we ask you, if you are an Individual, to **expressly** request it and to acknowledge that you have been informed of the consequences, using two separate boxes, not pre-ticked, before payment. Depending on the applicable law, performance started at your request may limit your right of withdrawal or require payment for the part already performed. Without this request we cannot activate access immediately: write to {{contact_email}}.
