# Privacy policy

Controller: **{{seller_name}}**, {{seller_address}} ({{seller_country}}). Contact for any question about your data: {{contact_email}}.

## 1. Data processed
- **Account**: e-mail address and password (stored hashed, never in clear).
- **Order and billing**: customer type (Individual or Business), name, billing address, company name and tax identifier where applicable, offer, amount, payment references.
- **Proof of acceptance**: date and time (UTC), language, version and fingerprint of the terms accepted, statement accepted, any request for immediate performance.
- **Content you create**: saved scenarios, folders, broker profile (name, logo), aircraft listings and enquiry messages.
- **Usage**: anonymous product events (no e-mail, name or free text) and technical security logs.

## 2. Purposes and bases
| Purpose | Basis |
|---|---|
| Provide the application, manage your account and orders | Performance of the contract |
| Invoice, keep accounts, keep proof of acceptance | Legal obligations and legitimate interest in proving the contract |
| Security, preventing abuse and quota circumvention | Legitimate interest |
| Audience measurement | Your consent, where the law requires it |
| Commercial information | Your consent, optional and separate |

Processing that is necessary for the contract does not rest on an optional consent.

## 3. Recipients
Providers that process data on our behalf: hosting ({{host_details}}), database (Supabase), payment and invoices (Stripe), e-mail delivery (Resend) and, depending on your consent choice, audience measurement (Google Analytics). The contact details of a prospective buyer who answers a listing are passed to the advertiser.

## 4. Transfers outside your country
Some providers may process data outside your country. The safeguards are those provided by the law that applies to the processing.

## 5. Retention
- Orders, proof of acceptance and invoices: {{retention_years}} years.
- Account and content: while the account is active, then deletion on request, subject to the retention obligations above.
- Listing enquiry messages: for the life of the listing, then deleted.

## 6. Your rights
Depending on applicable law: access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, portability, withdrawal of consent at any time, and complaint to the competent supervisory authority. Write to {{contact_email}}. Erasure does not extend to data we must keep (section 5).
